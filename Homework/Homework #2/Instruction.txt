Delivery Fee, Tax, and Total
 

A customer places an order with a local delivery service. In addition to the cost of the items, the company charges a 12% delivery fee and 7% sales tax.

Write a Python program that asks the user to enter the cost of the items ordered.

The program should then:

Calculate the 12% delivery fee based on the cost of the items.
Calculate the 7% sales tax based on the cost of the items.
Calculate the total amount of the order, including the item cost, delivery fee, and sales tax.
Display:
the original cost of the items,
the delivery fee,
the sales tax, and
the total amount of the order.
Example
Enter the cost of the items: 50.00
Item cost: $50.00
Delivery fee: $6.00
Sales tax: $3.50
Total: $59.50


Requirements
Store the item cost entered by the user in a variable.
Use calculations to determine the delivery fee and sales tax.
Store the calculated amounts in variables.
Calculate the final total.
Display each amount with a clear description.
Your program should work with amounts that include decimal values.
