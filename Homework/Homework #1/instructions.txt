A school club is holding a fundraiser. The company running the fundraiser gives the club 28% of the total amount of sales.

Write a Python program that:

Asks the user to enter the projected amount of total sales for the fundraiser.

Calculates the amount of money the school club will receive.

Displays the amount the club will receive.

 

Example
Enter the projected sales: 3500
The club will receive $980.00

 

Requirements
Store the projected sales amount in a variable.

Use a calculation to determine the club’s share of the sales.

Store the calculated amount in a variable.

Display the result in a clear message.

Your program should work with sales amounts that include decimal values.
